sha1 - vendored inside swift-nio

Source:  swift-nio/Sources/CNIOSHA1/c_nio_sha1.c
         swift-nio/Sources/CNIOSHA1/include/CNIOSHA1.h
Origin:  FreeBSD, itself from the KAME project (KAME: sha1.c,v 1.5 2000/11/08)
Pin:     swift-nio 0b18836bd8b0162e7e17a995a3fbee20ed8f3b2b

CNIOSHA1 is an ordinary target of the swift-nio package, so it is compiled and linked
whenever swift-nio is. Its license - BSD-3, held by the WIDE Project rather than by the
SwiftNIO authors - exists only as the comment block at the top of c_nio_sha1.c. swift-nio's
NOTICE.txt names the component and links to opensource.org's generic BSD-3-Clause template,
but it does not reproduce the text, and the WIDE Project copyright line appears nowhere in
any license file in the checkout. A file-name search for LICENSE/NOTICE/COPYING therefore
finds nothing that covers it, which is why this supplemental exists.

The text below is copied verbatim from the header of c_nio_sha1.c at the pinned revision.
